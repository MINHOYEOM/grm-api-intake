AI Disclosure

How this content is produced, and caveats

This service regularly collects and analyses public material from major regulators and authoritative expert bodies — FDA, EMA, MHRA, PIC/S, ICH, WHO, Health Canada, ECA, MFDS, and others — and presents it as briefing cards generated with generative AI.

Figures, codes, dates, quotations from the original text, source links, and machine-extracted tables are taken from the source and are displayed separately from the generative-AI summary.

Each item is classified by evidence grade (A, B, C) and signal tier (1–3) and comes with its source and a link to the official document, so you can trace and verify it against the primary material.

This service is reference material to support awareness of regulatory trends and quality issues. The summaries, analysis, and interpretation do not represent the views of any regulatory authority and must not be taken as legal, regulatory, quality, or compliance advice or as a certification opinion. Review important decisions against the official source and with professional advisers.
